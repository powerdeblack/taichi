How to Play

1. Build your squad

Pick 3 Axies from the roster (species can repeat). A species only decides the 3D model and the class triangle bonus (Beast>Plant>Aqua>Beast, Bird>Bug>Reptile>Bird) — it's the card set (Warrior/Priest/Mage/Ranger/Rogue/Shaman) you assign each Axie that decides its real cards. Pairing a species with its own native set (⭐, e.g. Bird+Ranger) unlocks a bonus signature card, but any pairing is playable. Give each Axie a 5-card loadout split across Attack/Defense/Heal however you like — that split is what makes it hit hard, tank, or heal. Mark exactly 1 Axie as Tank, then hit Start Duel.

2. Aiming and playing cards

The duel runs in real time — no turns, energy just keeps regenerating. Tap any Axie on the board (yours or the rival's), or its HP bar, to select it — a white ring shows the pick, and it stays selected until you tap a different Axie, so you can chain several cards on the same target — then tap a card in your hand to use it on that target. Attack cards need the target within range: short range only reaches an enemy within actual contact distance of your Axie right now, long range reaches anyone alive. Defense cards always go to your own squad: the ally you selected, or the one that needs it most (Thorns go on the Tank). Heal cards follow your 🎯: on an ally they heal (no 🎯 = the most hurt ally), on an enemy they become Reverse Heal and deal damage — the card turns red with “↩ REVERSE” so you know before you let go. Every card shows its numbers: ⚔️ damage, 💚 healing, 🛡️ protection.

3. Holding a card: range and misses

Press and hold a card to aim: a ring on the snow shows how far it reaches from the Axie that owns it, and a line shows who it will hit — your selected enemy if the card reaches it, otherwise the nearest enemy it does reach. Green = in range, red = out of range. The card fires when you let go; an attack released out of range misses and is spent, so walk closer first. Defense/Heal cards have no range limit.

Every card takes about 5 seconds: it charges on its Axie, flies to the target and only then lands. Until those 5 seconds pass you can't play another card. The rival plays by the same rule — watch the bar over its Axies to see its next hit coming.

4. Effects and archetypes

🩸 Bleed stacks up to 3 and deals 6 per stack every 2s tick for 3 ticks. ☠️ Poison adds 3 stacks per hit (max 6), deals 3 per stack each tick and fades by one. ⚔️ Damage is the direct hit of an attack card. 💚 Heal restores HP right away (stronger with more MP); Regen heals a little every tick. The Archetypes on the team screen are ready-made squads built around one of these — tap "Use this team" to load one, then tweak it if you like.

5. The Blizzard and the 3:20 limit

After 2 minutes a Blizzard hits every Axie each tick (harder every 15s) and halves all healing. The match always ends by 3:20: if both Tanks are still standing, the one with more HP left wins. The clock next to your energy counts down. Which team beats which is up to you to find out.

6. Damage, team cards and energy

Every attack's base damage is at least 20% of a reference Tank's HP (166 → 33); plain hits like Brutal Charge or Arcane Blast go higher, effect cards (Bleed, Poison, Deathmark, control…) sit on that floor. Each attack card in an Axie's loadout adds +2 attack to every hit it makes. Defense and Heal cards cover your whole team at full value (a Reverse Heal hits the whole enemy team); only Secrets and Thorns stay on one Axie. Attacks cost energy (2, heavy hits and Stuns 3) and every Heal gives +2 ⚡ back — healing is how you fund your next big hit.

Your hand: your 15 cards (5 per Axie) are shuffled into a deck; you hold 3 and draw a new one each time you play (Retain cards stay). When the deck runs out, the discard is shuffled back in. The rival plays by the exact same rules — its own deck and 3-card hand — and it reads the board like you do: it watches your cast bar, stuns casters, braces with defenses and saves energy for big cards.

7. Control and Secrets (like Axie Origin)

Every card shows its type: Attack (with its reach, 🗡️ short 2.3 or 🏹 long 6), Skill (defense or heal) or Secret. Each class has a control attack: 😵 Stun — the Axie can't play cards, a card it's charging is interrupted, and a stunned Tank can't move its squad. 🥶 Chill — no dodging and the squad moves at half speed. 😱 Fear — if that Axie attacks in the next 4s, the attack misses. Each class also has a ❓ Secret: laid face-down on an ally, it springs by itself when that Axie is attacked (strike back, freeze, stun, poison, dodge + fear, or heal when low). You see which Secret your own Axie holds; for the rival you only see that one is there. A Cleanse removes Stun, Chill and Fear.

8. Tank, Taunt, and movement

The Tank sits at the center and "taunts" — anyone attacking from close to it is forced to hit the Tank instead of picking freely, win or lose. Hold the joystick (on a computer: W A S D or the arrow keys) to walk the Tank anywhere on the snowfield; on a computer, cards work with the mouse: press a card to aim, release to play, drag off it (or press Esc) to cancel; the other 2 Axies escort it automatically, keeping formation around wherever it goes. Use "Move an Axie" (a short cooldown) to swap two of your own lanes instead — handy for ducking out of Taunt range or lining up a short-range hit.
