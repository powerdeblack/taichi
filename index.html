How to Play

1. Build your squad

Pick 3 Axies from the roster (species can repeat). A species only decides the 3D model and the class triangle bonus (Beast>Plant>Aqua>Beast, Bird>Bug>Reptile>Bird) — it's the card set (Warrior/Priest/Mage/Ranger/Rogue/Shaman) you assign each Axie that decides its real cards. Pairing a species with its own native set (⭐, e.g. Bird+Ranger) unlocks a bonus signature card, but any pairing is playable. Give each Axie a 5-card loadout split across Attack/Defense/Heal however you like — that split is what makes it hit hard, tank, or heal. Mark exactly 1 Axie as Tank, then hit Start Duel.

2. Aiming and playing cards

The duel runs in real time — no turns, energy just keeps regenerating. Tap any Axie on the board (yours or the rival's), or its HP bar, to select it — a white ring shows the pick, and it stays selected until you tap a different Axie, so you can chain several cards on the same target — then tap a card in your hand to use it on that target. Attack cards need the target within range: short range only reaches an enemy within actual contact distance of your Axie right now, long range reaches anyone alive. Defense/Heal cards work on either side: cast on an ally for the normal effect, or on an enemy to reverse it (a shield becomes Vulnerable, a heal becomes damage).

3. Holding a card: range and misses

Press and hold a card to aim: a ring on the snow shows how far it reaches from the Axie that owns it, and a line shows who it will hit — your selected enemy, or the nearest one if you haven't picked. Green = in range, red = out of range. The card fires when you let go; an attack released out of range misses and is spent, so walk closer first. Defense/Heal cards have no range limit.

4. Effects and archetypes

🩸 Bleed stacks up to 3 and deals 4 per stack every 2s tick for 3 ticks. ☠️ Poison adds 3 stacks per hit (max 9), deals 2 per stack each tick and fades by one. ⚔️ Damage is the direct hit of an attack card. 💚 Heal restores HP right away (stronger with more MP); Regen heals a little every tick. The Archetypes on the team screen are ready-made squads built around one of these — tap "Use this team" to load one, then tweak it if you like.

5. Tank, Taunt, and movement

The Tank sits at the center and "taunts" — anyone attacking from close to it is forced to hit the Tank instead of picking freely, win or lose. Hold the joystick to walk the Tank anywhere on the snowfield; the other 2 Axies escort it automatically, keeping formation around wherever it goes. Use "Move an Axie" (a short cooldown) to swap two of your own lanes instead — handy for ducking out of Taunt range or lining up a short-range hit.
