How to Play

1. Build your squad

Pick 3 Axies from the roster (species can repeat). A species only decides the 3D model and the class triangle bonus (Beast>Plant>Aqua>Beast, Bird>Bug>Reptile>Bird) — it's the card set (Warrior/Priest/Mage/Ranger/Rogue/Shaman) you assign each Axie that decides its real cards. Pairing a species with its own native set (⭐, e.g. Bird+Ranger) unlocks a bonus signature card, but any pairing is playable. Give each Axie a 5-card loadout split across Attack/Defense/Heal however you like — that split is what makes it hit hard, tank, or heal. Mark exactly 1 Axie as Tank, then hit Start Duel.

2. Aiming and playing cards

The duel runs in real time — no turns, energy just keeps regenerating. Tap any Axie on the board (yours or the rival's), or its HP bar, to select it — a white ring shows the pick — then tap a card in your hand to use it on that target. Attack cards need the target within range: short range only reaches an enemy within actual contact distance of your Axie right now, long range reaches anyone alive. Defense/Heal cards work on either side: cast on an ally for the normal effect, or on an enemy to reverse it (a shield becomes Vulnerable, a heal becomes damage).

3. Tank, Taunt, and movement

The Tank sits at the center and "taunts" — anyone attacking from close to it is forced to hit the Tank instead of picking freely, win or lose. Hold the joystick to roam the Tank around; the other 4 Axies escort it automatically, keeping formation around wherever it goes. Use "Move an Axie" (a short cooldown) to swap two of your own lanes instead — handy for ducking out of Taunt range or lining up a short-range hit.
