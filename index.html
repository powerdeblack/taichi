Freely pick 5 Axies (classes can repeat) and give each one a 5-card loadout of Attack/Defense/Heal — exactly 1 must be marked as Tank. Each Axie also picks a card set (Warrior, Priest, Mage, Ranger, Rogue, Shaman): that's what actually decides its cards, not its species. Pairing a species with its own native set (⭐, e.g. Bird+Ranger) unlocks a bonus signature card. The duel's goal is to kill the rival's Tank.

Concept prototype for the Vibeathon — a big Lunacia dueling hall (stone floor, a ring of columns, a glowing lunar sigil at the center), real-time, no turns: both squads walk in from a distance when the duel starts, both sides regenerate energy continuously and can play cards anytime. Tap any Axie on the board (yours or the rival's), or its HP bar directly, to select it — glowing white ring — then tap a card in your hand to use it on that target: attack cards only work on a selected enemy in range, defense/heal cards work on either side (ally = normal effect, enemy = reversed — Vulnerable instead of a shield, damage instead of healing). Every hit/heal now bursts a 3D impact effect at the point of contact, and floating damage/heal text lingers longer so the action's easier to follow. The Tank sits in the center with a taunt radius: attack from the front line and you're forced to hit it. Hold the joystick to roam the Tank freely — the other 4 escort it, staying in formation around wherever it goes — or use "Move an Axie" (a few seconds' cooldown) to swap any two of your own.
